---
name: Manual spam handling
description: Corvlio's owner-controlled spam classification and caller alert suppression boundary.
---

Spam classification is manual by the business owner. Marking a lead hides it from ordinary lead lists and suppresses future alerts for that normalized caller number, while keeping the lead recoverable.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested an owner action with an easy undo and prohibited automatic call blocking.

**How to apply:** Keep spam marking reversible and owner-controlled. Do not reject or block calls automatically.
