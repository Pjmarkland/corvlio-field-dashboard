---
name: SMS alert consent boundary
description: Corvlio's SMS owner-alert consent and delivery limits.
---

SMS alert consent is optional during onboarding. Only consenting business owners may receive lead texts about their own business; callers are never texted. Owners who do not opt in continue to receive the existing push and email alerts. Do not activate SMS delivery until Twilio A2P approval is in place.

**Why:** Consent was added to prepare the A2P registration, while no owner SMS alert sender is currently active.

**How to apply:** Keep onboarding consent separate from required legal agreements, and enforce affirmative consent and owner-number checks at any future SMS sending boundary.
