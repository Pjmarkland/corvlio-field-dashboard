---
name: Private lead notes
description: Owner-written lead notes are separate from caller intake and must remain internal by default.
---

Private lead notes are owner-side business information, not caller-provided intake. Do not include them in caller communications or AI/provider context without explicit owner approval.

**Why:** The user requested private, per-lead owner notes, separate from the stored call details.

**How to apply:** When adding exports, notifications, integrations, or AI features, treat sharing owner notes as a separate permission decision; exclude them by default.
