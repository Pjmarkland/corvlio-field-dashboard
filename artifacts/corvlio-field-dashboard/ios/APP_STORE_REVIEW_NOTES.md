# App Store Review Notes

**App:** Corvlio Field Dashboard  
**Bundle ID:** `com.corvlio.fielddashboard`  
**Current project version:** 1.0 (build 1)

## Account access

Use the dedicated reviewer account and credentials entered in App Store Connect's Review Information. The account should be linked to a separate Corvlio business containing admin-prepared fictional sample data. Do not use an existing client's account. The app has no shared demo login or authentication bypass; do not put account credentials in this file.

## Review flow

1. Sign in with the App Store Connect review account.
2. Review the lead dashboard and Settings.
3. Open **Settings → Billing** to see the read-only subscription status summary. The iOS app has no checkout, payment, plan-selection, or Stripe portal action. Website billing remains on the website.
4. Open the Support page from Settings, or visit its public URL below.
5. Account deletion is available in Settings. It permanently removes the review account and its associated app data; use only the dedicated review account to test this.

Push notifications are optional and can be enabled from Settings. The app remains usable if notifications are declined.
